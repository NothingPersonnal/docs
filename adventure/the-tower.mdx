---
title: "The Tower"
description: "Twelve floors, three bosses."
icon: "chess-rook"
---

The Tower has twelve floors, cleared in order, on a map every player shares, with bosses on floors 4, 8 and 12. Clear a floor to open the next, and run any floor you have already cleared again. Each run costs one Sceau.
