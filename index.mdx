Sonder is a dark fantasy town and dungeon game for your phone. You build a walled town of eleven buildings, you pick who goes in, and you send your heroes to farm, delve dungeons and climb a Tower of twelve floors.

Once the fight starts, they decide. Every hero walks in with a past and nine traits of their own, and those traits choose when they charge, heal, loot or run.

<Note>
  Sonder is in closed alpha. It is free on mobile and access is by invite. [Join the alpha on playsonder.app](https://playsonder.app).
</Note>

## How a day in Sonder goes

<Steps>
  <Step title="Build the town">
    Your town is home. Peasants work the Trades, your heroes rest, train and make friends, and the cemetery is where the fallen come back.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Farm and delve">
    Scouts find dungeons that stay open for a few hours. Pick the party and their gear, then send them in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch them choose">
    Inside, you do not steer. Each hero reads the fight through their own personality and acts on it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Loot, craft and climb">
    Workshops turn what your heroes bring back into gear. Then you take the strongest party up the Tower.
  </Step>
</Steps>
