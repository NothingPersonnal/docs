Your town is eleven buildings on a walled island: the Blacksmith, Alchemist, Weaver, Jeweler and Tanner workshops, the Inn, the Dormitory, the Training Ground, the Cemetery, the Synthesis Shrine and the Dungeon Portal. Peasants keep the workshops running, your heroes live there between runs, and the Cemetery is where the fallen come back.
